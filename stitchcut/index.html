StitchCut – Video Merger

Merge clips and photos into one video, trim, speed up, add text and music, and export in HD. Everything runs on your device: no account, no uploads.

Merge videos & photos

Pick several videos and photos, mix them in any order and join them into one video.

Trim, split & speed

Cut the parts you don't need, split clips at the playhead and speed them up or slow them down.

Filters, text & music

Apply one-tap filters, adjust brightness and color, add captions and a music track.

Every social format

Fit your video to 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9 for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and YouTube, and export in up to 1080p.

Available for iPhone, iPad and Android. Works offline.
