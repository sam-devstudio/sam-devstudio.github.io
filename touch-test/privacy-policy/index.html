Privacy Policy

Display Tester – Touch Screen Test · Effective September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Future Apps Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the Display Tester – Touch Screen Test app (the "App") for iPhone, iPad and Android. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • No account, sign-up or personal details are needed to use the App.
  • We do not collect, store or sell your personal information on our own servers.
  • Your settings stay on your device.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage data to deliver and measure ads, as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves your preferences — theme, language and whether reminder notifications are on — locally on your device. This information is never sent to us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Screen tests (touch, multi-touch, color) and Display info run entirely on your device. Touch positions and display details are not recorded or transmitted.

2. Permissions

  • Notifications — used only to show an optional weekly reminder to test your screen. Reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the App's Settings or in your device settings.
  • Internet access — used to load ads and, on Android, app configuration and update checks.
  • Tracking (iPhone and iPad only) — the App may ask for permission to use your device's advertising identifier for personalized ads. Saying no does not limit any App feature.

The App does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information according to their own privacy policies:

Google AdMob (advertising) — iPhone, iPad and Android

AdMob shows banner and full-screen ads. To deliver, personalize (where permitted) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, taps) and diagnostic data

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google User Messaging Platform (consent) — iPhone, iPad and Android

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the App uses Google's consent form to ask whether ads may be personalized. Your choice is stored on your device and shared with Google's ad services.

Google Firebase (Android only)

The Android version uses Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the App is used (for example, which screens are opened and app crashes), and Firebase Remote Config to update app settings without a new release. Firebase may collect app instance identifiers, device information and usage events. See Firebase privacy information.

Google Play services (Android only)

The Android version uses Google Play's in-app update service to tell you when a new version is available.

4. How information is used

Information collected by the services above is used to show and measure ads, keep the App free, understand and improve App performance, and prevent fraud and abuse. We do not use it to identify you personally, and we do not sell personal information.

6. Data retention

We do not keep any of your data on our servers. Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the App. Data collected by third-party services is kept according to their policies.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal information ourselves, most requests are best handled through the choices above or directly with Google. You can also contact us and we will help.

9. Security

We rely on the security of your device's operating system for data stored locally, and third-party services transmit data using encrypted connections.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
