Frequently asked questions

How does the Touch test work?

Open Touch and slide one finger across the screen. Each dot you touch changes color. The test passes when every dot has been touched. If some dots never change no matter how often you touch them, that area of your touch screen may not be responding.

I can't complete the Touch test
  • Move your finger slowly and make sure you cover the very edges and corners of the grid.
  • Remove thick screen protectors or cases that cover the edge of the screen, then try again.
  • Clean the screen and dry your fingers.
  • Tap the X button to leave the test at any time.
How many fingers does the Multi-touch test detect?

The app shows every touch point your device reports. The maximum is set by your device's hardware and operating system, not by the app, so it differs between phones and tablets.

How do I check for dead or stuck pixels?

Open Color and tap anywhere to change the full-screen color. Look closely at each color: a dot that stays black is usually a dead pixel, and a dot that stays one bright color is usually a stuck pixel. Turn your screen brightness up for the best results.

Why does Display info show "≈" next to the screen size?

Some systems don't report the physical screen size directly. For newer or unrecognized devices the app estimates it from the resolution and pixel density, and marks the value with "≈".

Why does the refresh rate show 60 Hz on my 120 Hz device?

The app shows the maximum refresh rate the system reports. Low Power Mode and some accessibility settings (such as Limit Frame Rate) cap the display at 60 Hz. Turn these off and reopen Display info.

How do I change the language or theme?

Go to Settings. Choose Language to pick one of 18 languages, or Theme for Light, Dark or System default.

How do I turn reminder notifications on or off?

Use the Notification switch in Settings. On iPhone and iPad, if notifications were blocked earlier, allow them in the device's Settings → Notifications → Display Tester.

Why are there ads? Can I limit ad tracking?

Ads keep the app free. On iPhone and iPad you can stop personalized ads based on tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does the app collect my personal data?

The app doesn't ask for an account or personal information. Your settings are stored only on your device. Our advertising partner, Google AdMob, collects some device data to show ads; see the Privacy Policy.

How can I rate the app?

Open More → Rate us. It takes you to the app's page on the App Store or Google Play. Thank you, reviews really help!
